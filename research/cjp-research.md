# CJP / Gyanesh Kumar: factual research brief

**Checked 11 October 2026.** This is source-backed editorial research, not a statement on behalf of CJP. Quoted accusations and demands below describe the speakers' positions; they are not findings of electoral wrongdoing.

## Identity, spelling and context

- The organization in this protest is **Cockroach Janta Party (CJP)**, a youth-led, originally satirical movement/pressure group; it is **not registered as a political party**. Its own site uses “Cockroach Janta Party” as the organization name. “Cockroach Janata Party” appears as an *alternate romanization* in that site's structured metadata, but **Janta** is the displayed English brand spelling. The Hindi name is **कॉकरोच जनता पार्टी**. Do not silently replace “Janta” with “Janata” in the headline. [S1, S2]
- A **different** organization, **Citizens for Justice and Peace**, also abbreviates its name CJP; its own site describes itself as a human-rights movement. Do not confuse the two. [S3]
- The user's word **“Gyanesh” is correctly spelled** in reporting about **Gyanesh Kumar**, Chief Election Commissioner (CEC). No spelling error is identifiable in that supplied word. Use the full name and title on first mention. The founder's name appears as **Abhijeet Dipke** on the movement's site and in the BBC, although some other reports spell his given name “Abhijit”; prefer Abhijeet when naming him. [S1, S2, S4]

## What is happening and why

- This **October 2026** campaign is for electoral-roll accountability, electoral reforms, and Kumar's resignation. It followed *The Indian Express* investigation reporting that fellow Election Commissioners **Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi** formally objected at least **14 times in 10 months** to decisions about additions/deletions and voter-roll systems during the **Special Intensive Revision (SIR)**. CJP alleges the process endangers eligible voters and the integrity of elections. The Election Commission says SIR is intended to remove ineligible/duplicate entries and **denies** accusations of manipulation; allegations of partisan voter-roll rigging must be attributed, not asserted as fact. [S4, S5, S6, S7]
- The movement's **three demand groups** in its petition are: **(1)** Kumar resign within 48 hours, criminal proceedings and an answer to who gave instructions; **(2)** pause upcoming polls, freeze SIR, restore the January 2025 voter list, commission an independent inquiry and ask the Supreme Court to seize SIR files; **(3)** repeal the 2023 election-commissioner appointment law and enact a new law with civil-society participation. These are **CJP proposals, not adopted government policy or judicial orders**. [S8; independently summarized by S7]
- The latest reporting available on 11 October describes Delhi protests over the weekend, detentions, release of Dipke and two other leaders on Sunday, and their statement that protests would continue. This is a developing situation; avoid fixed turnout/detention counts as evergreen copy. [S9]
- **Earlier outcome:** A five-week summer protest concerned exam paper leaks, education reforms and Education Minister **Dharmendra Pradhan's** resignation. He resigned on **25 July 2026**; CJP called off that protest and celebrated it as a victory. That verifies the resignation and the group's reaction, **not** completion of all its examination-reform demands or proof that every subsequent reform was enacted. The current electoral campaign is distinct. [S10]

## Proposed factual website copy

**Heading:** “Cockroach Janta Party: the campaign for electoral-roll accountability”

**Body:** “Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) is a youth-led satirical movement, not a registered political party. In October 2026, it has called for electoral reforms and the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar amid controversy over the Special Intensive Revision of voter lists. Its petition calls for an independent inquiry, a pause and rollback of the revision, and changes to the law governing the appointment of election commissioners. The Election Commission and BJP reject allegations that the revision is being manipulated for partisan advantage. In July, CJP ended an earlier protest about examinations after Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan resigned.” [S2, S4, S8, S10]

**Separate satire disclaimer:** “This is an independent satirical/community project about the protest. It is not an official Cockroach Janta Party channel or an endorsement by its organizers. Satirical commentary is labeled separately from sourced news and CJP's stated demands.”

## Hold until independently confirmed

- That Kumar personally ordered illegal deletion, committed a crime, or rigged an election; distinguish accusations from established findings. The reported internal objections alone do not establish these propositions. [S4–S6]
- That the January 2025 rolls were restored, SIR frozen, future polls paused, the 2023 appointment law repealed, or Kumar resigned; these are **demands**, not verified outcomes. [S8–S9]
- That **all** July examination reforms passed or that Pradhan's resignation alone proves direct causal responsibility for a specific policy change. [S10]
- Exact current detention, turnout, voter-deletion or petition-signature totals without a dated, independently verified definition and timestamp; draft-list deletions and final exclusions are not interchangeable. [S4–S5, S9]
- Official affiliation, permission to use CJP branding, founder endorsement, ownership of any of the many similarly named domains, or a claim that this project takes action for CJP; no such authorization was established here. [S1]
